# Disclosure and legal pointers

Not legal advice. Involve counsel in every jurisdiction where the token is offered or traded.

## Publish (always)
- Total and circulating supply, the genesis allocation table, vesting schedules.
- The issuance formula with bounds, plus fee policy (burn and distribution).
- The reward split and how each role earns (verified work, stake).
- Slashing rules, commission limits.
- Treasury spending rules and who controls the treasury.
- Who can change each parameter, the process, and the timelock.

## EU: MiCA (checked 2026-11-25)
- Offering crypto-assets to the public in the EU, or seeking admission to trading, generally needs a crypto-asset **white paper**. Its content and form depend on the token type (other crypto-assets, ARTs, EMTs).
- Format: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2984. White papers in **XHTML with iXBRL tagging** became mandatory on **23 Dec 2025**. ESMA published the XBRL taxonomy on 5 Aug 2025, with entry points for ARTs, EMTs and other crypto-assets.
- The offeror or issuer is solely responsible for the content. ESMA's example tools are voluntary.
- ESMA's interim white-paper register was due to move into ESMA's systems by mid-2026, so check the current status.
- Trading platforms (CASPs) that seek admission themselves must comply when no white paper exists (ESMA Q&A 2552).

## Elsewhere
Token classification rules differ (securities, commodities, payment tokens). Check before any sale, airdrop, or exchange listing.
